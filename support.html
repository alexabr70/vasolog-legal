About VasoLog

VasoLog is a personal symptom diary for Raynaud's Phenomenon. Log each attack - severity, color phase, affected fingers, triggers - with the weather captured automatically, and generate a clear PDF report for your doctor. All health data is stored locally on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
All your attack records, photos, and notes stay on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app removes this data permanently.
How does the weather get attached to an attack?
When you log an attack, the app fetches the current temperature, humidity, pressure, and wind for your location from the Open-Meteo service. Your coordinates are used only to retrieve weather and are not stored on any server.
How do I create a report for my doctor?
Open the Report screen, pick a date range, and export a PDF. You can share it with your rheumatologist, vascular specialist, or GP.
Which languages are supported?
The app interface is available in multiple languages and follows your device language automatically.
How do I delete all my data?
Uninstall the app. Because all data is stored locally, removing the app deletes everything permanently.
